# Social Hub, Guild, Leaderboards, Pet Market, Chat, Cosmetics, Mini Games

This release adds the social layer to Pet Star: Trading Duel. Collection, 5-card packs, evolution, Battle 3v3 and both currencies are unchanged. The Admin Panel UI is not changed: only APIs and data for a future panel were added.

## Lobby

There are five hexagon buttons in a 2 + 3 honeycomb:

| Button | Colour |
|---|---|
| Chợ Giao Dịch (Pet Market) | purple |
| Trò Chơi Khác (Mini Games) | blue |
| Sảnh Liên Thông (Pet World) | teal |
| Bắt Đầu (Start / Battle 3v3) | gold, largest |
| Social Hub | starry pink-violet |

- **Art:** each hexagon emblem is original art: `ui_hex_*`, generated by the project pipeline.
- **Labels:** sit in a safe band inside the hexagon, can wrap to two lines, and are never scaled.
- **Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (SIL OFL), shipped as woff2 in `web-client/public/fonts`.
- **Chat:** a 3-channel chat dock sits bottom-left. When it is open, the Trainer stage moves so the chat never covers the Trainer, the Pet or the bottom bar.

## Server (`backend/src/PetStar.Api`)

Migration: `20261123062412_SocialHubGuildV2`.
- It only adds tables and columns, and fills in sensible defaults for existing rows.
- Existing tables are extended where an equivalent already existed:
  - `feed_posts`, `feed_comments`, `guilds`, `guild_members`, `chat_messages`, `trainer_profiles`, `trainer_outfits`, `market_*`.
- New tables:
  - `feed_comment_likes`, `moderation_queue`, `social_settings`;

**Feed rules**
- Visibility: public, friends, guild or private. The server checks it for reading, liking, commenting and sharing.
- Likes: one per account (primary key), concurrent likes cannot double-count, and a like notification is sent only once per liker.
- Replies: one level only.
- Shares: keep their source. Friends-only and private posts cannot be shared; guild posts can only be shared inside the guild.
- Pet Showcase: cards come from the database, so the client cannot fake rarity or ownership.
- Weekly spotlight: counts only distinct other players (likes ×3, comments ×2).
- Auto-moderation holds risky text as `pending`: payment, off-platform, adult, harassment and personal-info patterns.
- Three distinct reports hide content and put it in the review queue.
- Rate limits and duplicate checks apply.
- Edits are allowed within 24 hours and are moderated again.

**Guild rules**
- Each guild has a tag, emblem, banner, visibility (public, or private = invite-only) and minimum level.
- Roles: leader (Guild Master) > vice > officer > elite > member. You can only manage lower ranks.
- Guild EXP and level raise the member cap.
- Missions advance from real actions (PvE/PvP, pack openings, evolutions, posts, boss attacks, showcases). Each member claims once per period.
- Guild Boss:
  - your saved team fights the boss formation for 8 rounds on the shared 3v3 engine (AI on both sides, server seed);
  - damage is read from the engine's event log and subtracted from the shared HP under a row lock;
  - 3 attacks per day.
- Guild War:
  - guilds register each week and are paired by closest level;
  - each battle is a playable 3v3 match against an opponent member's saved team;
  - win +3, draw +1 point; the week settles lazily and rewards are idempotent.
- Dissolving needs the guild name typed to confirm, and the leader must be the last member. Guild posts are deleted, never made public.

**Leaderboard rules**
- 17 boards; ties break on the lower id.
- Social boards count only distinct likers, never the author, and ignore accounts younger than 24 hours.
- Season close takes snapshots of the top 100; the top 10 receive rewards and the top 3 receive a frame.

**Market rules**
- Trades are card-for-card with no currency.
- On accept, all cards are locked in id order and re-validated (owner, lock, busy, active match), then both sides move in one transaction.
- On failure the offer is marked failed and nothing moves.
- Teams and companions that used a traded card are cleared.

**Shop rules**
- Prices come from `cosmetics.json` and `trainers.json`, in in-game currencies only.
- Skins are sold only when all three art frames exist (`assetsComplete`).
- Daily limits apply to item bundles, and guild bubbles require a guild.
- Cosmetics never change PvP stats.

**Chat rules**
- Bubbles and frames are added from the sender's profile.
- Risky messages are held and not broadcast.
- System messages and internal post links cannot be forged.
- Unread markers track what each player has read.

**Mini game rules**
- The server makes the board from its own seed and keeps the clock.
- It replays the moves, taps or answers and computes the score.
- It rejects games finished too fast, incomplete boards and auto-clickers (taps under 55 ms or perfectly regular).
- 3 rewarded plays per day, plus 1 Daily Challenge.

## Interactive Preview

The Preview is a static page. Its "Preview server" (`web-client/src/preview/world.ts`, stored in localStorage) mirrors the rules above, so real interaction is possible without a hosted server.

- **Accounts:** each tab signs in as one account. Create a second account (Settings → Log out → another name), or open a second tab, to friend, like, comment, share, trade, chat and join guilds with yourself.
- **No fake data:** the feed, market, guild list and leaderboards contain only what Preview accounts did. No NPC posts, no seeded listings, no fake rivals.
- **Guild Boss** damage is computed with the same TypeScript 3v3 engine (event-log damage).
- **Market** listings and trade offers escrow the cards. Proceeds and traded cards reach the other account through an inbox.
- **Not in the Preview:** image upload in posts, an online multi-player server, and the admin moderation UI.
